Welcome to Turning Point Church

Turning Point Church is a Christian community based in the Woodlands area of Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. We are dedicated to helping people build a real relationship with Jesus Christ while offering a warm and supportive environment for individuals and families.

Our Mission

We exist to encourage spiritual growth, strengthen families and equip our members to serve the wider community through fellowship, teaching and outreach.
