Our History

Turning Point Church has its roots in the 1950s, when a couple living in the Woodlands area of Durban felt called to support children in their community. What started as small meetings in a garage grew into a full congregation, first known as Woodlands Baptist Church.

In 1984 the church became New Covenant Fellowship Durban (NCF Durban), joining the wider New Covenant Ministries movement. It was officially registered as a non-profit organisation in 2004 and later became known as Turning Point Church, the name we carry today.

Our Mission

To help people build a meaningful relationship with Jesus Christ, while providing a friendly and supportive atmosphere for individuals and families through fellowship, teaching, leadership development and community outreach.

Our Vision

To be a church centred on Christ that strengthens families, develops responsible leaders and makes a genuine difference in the lives of our members and the community around us.

Who We Welcome

We serve individuals, families and young people across Durban who are looking for spiritual growth, real relationships and a supportive church community, no matter their background or stage of life.
